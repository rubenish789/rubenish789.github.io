About me

Robotics Engineer |
Computer Vision & Machine Learning

I am a final-year Robotics and Mechatronics student at KBTU (Kazakh-British Technical University) in Almaty. I work where hardware meets intelligence: robot arms that see with depth cameras, models that find defects on a production line, and small robots controlled over Wi-Fi or by voice.

My toolkit spans both sides — ROS 2, robot kinematics and embedded Linux on one end, Python, PyTorch, YOLO and OpenCV on the other. During my internship at Eltex I worked on Wi-Fi indoor positioning (RTLS), from access-point configuration to RSSI-based trilateration.

I'm looking for opportunities in robotics perception, computer vision and edge ML.
